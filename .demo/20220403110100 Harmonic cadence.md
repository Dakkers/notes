# Harmonic cadence

A **harmonic cadence** is a kind of statement made through harmonic movement that occurs at the end of a [[20220403104100 Phrase|phrase]].

Kinds of cadences:

- **Perfect Authentic** ([[20221224114300 Perfect authentic cadence|PAC]]):
  - `V - I` or `V7 - I`
  - Both chords are in root position
- **Imperfect Authentic** ([[20221224114400 Imperfect authentic cadence|IAC]]):
  - `V - I` or `V7 - I`
  - One or both chords are NOT in root position
- **Deceptive Cadence**:
  - `V - VIm` or `V7 - VIm`
  - Called "deceptive" because V or V7 makes it seem as if we're going to `I`
- **Half-Cadence**:
  - Phrase ends on `V`
  - Called "half" because it's half of an authentic cadence
- **Plagal**:
  - `IV - I`
  - Called plagal because of its similarity to the "amen" sound
