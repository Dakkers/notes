# Caplin Ahead!

A quick heads-up before charting these waters: much of this vault leans on
[[caplin-2013|William Caplin]]'s _form-functional_ vocabulary. Where older theory
labels a chord by what it **is**, Caplin labels it by what it **does** — its role
in a phrase.

Three functions steer almost everything:

- **Initiating** — begins an idea (tonic, the home port)
- **Medial** — keeps it moving (predominant, the open sea)
- **Concluding** — brings it to rest (dominant → tonic, back to harbour)

Keep these three in view and the progressions ahead read like a map rather than a
list.
